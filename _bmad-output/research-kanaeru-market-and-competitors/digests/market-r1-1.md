## A2. Generative AI adoption among SMBs and individuals, and SaaS market size

- **Claim:** Individual generative AI use in Japan (experience of use) was **58.8% in the FY2025 survey, up from 26.7% in FY2024**. The figure is 52.2% for ages 20+, which is comparable with the earlier surveys because the 2025 survey added respondents aged 15–19. Text-based AI drove the rise (55.0%). China was 93.6%.
  Source: https://www.soumu.go.jp/johotsusintokei/whitepaper/ja/r08/text/nd111110.txt ; https://internet.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2127686.html | 総務省 令和8年版情報通信白書 | 2026-07 | confidence: high | class: adoption
- **Claim:** In the FY2024 survey (令和7年版), 26.7% of individuals had used generative AI, and 44.7% of people in their 20s had. After "not needed for my life or work," the most common reason for not using it was **「使い方がわからない」**.
  Source: https://www.soumu.go.jp/johotsusintokei/whitepaper/ja/r07/html/nd112210.html | 総務省 令和7年版情報通信白書 | 2025-07 | confidence: high (primary; figures from the search summary of that page) | class: adoption/behavior
- **Claim:** Companies (FY2025 survey): 68.9% have a policy of using generative AI (actively, or in limited areas), up from 49.7%. That is **74.0% of large firms vs 58.1% of SMEs**. 86.4% use it in some business task, and about 70% of those use it for meeting minutes and email drafting. 27.0% have no organizational effort, a share that is higher among SMEs. The top expected benefit is efficiency through time savings (61.6%).
  Source: https://www.soumu.go.jp/johotsusintokei/whitepaper/ja/r08/html/nd121110.html ; .../nd121120.html ; .../nd121130.html | 総務省 令和8年版情報通信白書 | 2026-07 | confidence: high (primary pages; numbers from the search summary of those pages) | class: adoption
- **Claim:** 帝国データバンク (survey 2026-03, n=10,312): 34.5% of firms use generative AI in their work. That is **large firms 46.5%, SMEs 32.4%, small firms 28.0%**, and 86.7% of users report a benefit. The main problems are accuracy, **lack of know-how**, and defining the scope of use, along with a widening skill gap between employees.
  Source: https://aismiley.co.jp/ai_news/teikoku-databank-ai-202603report/ (secondary coverage of the TDB release) | 帝国データバンク (via AIsmiley) | 2026-04 (est.) | confidence: medium-high | class: adoption
- **Claim:** 東京商工リサーチ (2026-03/04, n=6,327): 20.3% of firms promote generative AI use as a company, and 37.5% have no policy.
  Source: https://www.tsr-net.co.jp/data/detail/1202766_1527.html (from the search summary) | TSR | 2026-04 | confidence: medium | class: adoption
- **Claim:** Adoption figures vary widely with the definition. "Any use" gives 86% (総務省), "used in work" gives 32% for SMEs (TDB), "company-promoted" gives 20% (TSR), and SME organizational process adoption gives about 12% (Leach, a small private survey). The gap between individuals experimenting and organizations deploying is the consistent signal.
  Source: as above, plus Leach 中小企業AI導入実態調査2026 (via the search summary; low confidence) | various | 2026 | confidence: medium | class: adoption
- **Claim:** MM総研 (2025-09) put individual generative AI usage experience at 21.8%, and found that people choose tools by context (Copilot for work or school, Grok for private use). This conflicts with 総務省's 26.7%/58.8%. **要確認: the definitions and samples differ.**
  Source: https://webtan.impress.co.jp/n/2025/09/25/50092 | MM総研 via Web担当者Forum | 2025-09 | confidence: medium | class: adoption
- **Claim:** Japan's SaaS/PaaS market (富士キメラ総研, 2025 edition) is forecast to reach **¥3兆3,975億 in FY2029 (+73.0% vs FY2024)** across 53 enterprise software categories. A composite estimate from an aggregator (Fuji Chimera, IDC, and ITR combined) gives about ¥1.45兆 for 2024.
  Source: https://aurant-technologies.com/blog/saas-market-japan-overall-survey-2026/ ; https://www.crex-data.com/industry/media-it/saas/market-size | aggregators citing 富士キメラ総研 | 2025–2026 | confidence: medium | class: size/growth | **要確認: primary 富士キメラ release not fetched; the 2026 edition was scheduled for 2026-07**

## A3. Pricing norms and willingness to pay

- **Claim:** About 80% of respondents use some subscription, and **monthly spend of ≤¥2,000 is the most common band** (ゼニエモン survey, 2025-07/08, n=400, ages 10+).
  Source: https://www.commercepick.com/archives/70690 | ゼニエモン via CommercePick | 2025-08/09 | confidence: low-medium (small sample) | class: pricing/behavior
- **Claim:** LINEリサーチ (2025-10, n=3,148, ages 15–69): nearly 60% use a flat-rate subscription, mostly video and music streaming.
  Source: https://www.commercepick.com/archives/77378 | LINEリサーチ via CommercePick | 2025-10/11 | confidence: medium | class: behavior
- **Claim:** Appliv/ナイル (published 2024-03): users hold 2.3 subscriptions on average, and the most common total monthly spend is under ¥3,000.
  Source: via the search summary of https://dime.jp/genre/1753924 | ナイル | 2024-03 | confidence: low-medium | class: pricing | 要確認: older than 2 years once 2026-03 passed; borderline
- **Claim:** Share of individuals paying for generative AI: the only Japanese figure found is **about 5% (GMO Research, 2023-12, n=1,126)**, which is stale. No 2025–2026 Japanese paid-share figure was found. ChatGPT's Japan MAU was estimated at 26.7M in 2026-05 (ヴァリューズ log panel), but without a paid split.
